Hi, I'm Hyun Seo, and most people call me Emily. I'm a biomedical engineer drawn to problems where imaging, machine learning, and real clinical need meet. What keeps me motivated is the gap between a method that works in a paper and a device a patient can actually use, and I like living in that gap.

I grew up speaking Korean and English, studied Biomedical Engineering and Computer Science at Johns Hopkins, and stayed on for my master's and now my PhD. Along the way I've worked across the full stack of medical devices: optics and hardware, signal processing, machine learning, and the human side of whether people will actually adopt a technology.
